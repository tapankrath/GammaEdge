How this works: the recommendation logic and every number shown is computed with the exact Black-Scholes formula in your browser (same engine as FairValue) — not AI-estimated. AI is only used to look up the two real unknowns: live spot price and implied volatility, both editable after the first lookup. The choice between a long option and a debit spread is a rule, not a guess: if your target move exceeds what IV is currently pricing in, a long option captures more of that excess; if your target is within the normal expected range, a debit spread reduces cost since you don't need the extra leverage.
