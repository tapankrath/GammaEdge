04  Recommendation
How the strikes are built: each leg's distance from spot is price × IV × √(DTE/365) × z(target delta), a standard-deviation approximation of where that delta actually sits — the same shortcut many desks use to eyeball strikes before pulling up a chain. It is not a live options price feed. Confirm actual delta, mid-price, and liquidity on your broker before placing anything. This tool is educational, not financial advice.
