GammaEdge Guide
9 standalone tools + 5 tabs inside the main dashboard. What each one does, and which one to reach for.
Quick Answers
"I have one ticker, want a full options strategy — where do I start?"
Got a chart screenshot? → ChartAI (reads the pattern). Want AI-researched live picks? → StockAI. Want instant formula-based comparison of 5 strategies with no AI wait? → StratEdge tab. Earnings coming up or sustained high IV? → StrategyAI.
"I already have open positions — what do I do with them?"
PortfolioAI for a specific adjustment plan (roll/close/hedge) on positions you paste or photograph. OptionsAI → Portfolio Risk Audit for a whole-book health check (Greeks, concentration, worst-case).
"I want steady weekly/monthly income, not a one-off trade."
WeeklyAI for a full AI-generated weekly plan with risk tiers. Alloc tab for CSP/covered-call yield comparisons. OptionsAI → Weekly Income Generator for a lighter-weight version of the same idea.
"I have a growth target and a deadline — what return do I actually need?"
CompoundAI — reverse-engineers the required per-period rate, tells you honestly if it's realistic, tracks your actual progress period by period.
"I want a lottery-ticket / huge-upside idea."
MoonshotAI for stock candidates with 10x+ equity potential. Lottery tab for a single OTM option's payoff table on a move you already have in mind.
"Is this specific strike actually priced right?"
FairValue — real Black-Scholes math (not AI-estimated), with live spot + IV lookup.
Standalone Tools (separate pages, linked from the grid)
ChartAI AI + Vision
Upload a chart screenshot, get 3 tiered strategies
▾
What it does
Drop in a chart screenshot (or several, for multi-timeframe). Claude reads the pattern visually and returns Conservative / Moderate / Aggressive strategies, each with DTE calibrated to your chart's timeframe (e.g. a daily chart gets 60/30/14 DTE; a 5-min chart gets 3/1/0 DTE), plus bear/base/bull P&L scenarios, probability of profit, and breakeven.
Best for
You're looking at a chart and want a strategy shaped around what you're seeing — support/resistance, a pattern, momentum — rather than a raw ticker lookup.
Open ChartAI →
StockAI AI + Live Data
Single-ticker AI forecast with live options chain
▾
What it does
Pick a ticker, pick a horizon (Next Day / Week / Month). Pulls live price, options chain, flow signals, and news, then generates a forecast with entry/strategy. Tracks your picks as paper trades and keeps a calibration history so you can see how the forecasts have actually performed.
Best for
Deep dive on one specific ticker, with a track record you can look back on.
Open StockAI →
OptionsAI AI + Web Search
4 tools: scanner, earnings, risk audit, weekly income
▾
What it does
Four separate AI-driven tools in one page: High Probability Scanner (market-wide candidates for the next 30 days), Earnings Options Strategy (pre/post-earnings plan for one ticker), Portfolio Risk Audit (paste positions, get Greeks/concentration/worst-case), and Weekly Income Strategy Generator (CSP/covered call/credit spread ideas for one week).
Best for
A lighter-weight, prompt-driven version of several other tools' jobs, useful when you want a quick take rather than the deeper single-purpose version (StrategyBuilder, PortfolioAI, WeeklyAI).
Open OptionsAI →
FairValue Pure Math
Real Black-Scholes pricing for any strike/DTE
▾
What it does
Enter ticker, strike, DTE, call/put. The actual price is computed with the exact Black-Scholes formula in your browser — verified against textbook reference values. AI is only used for the two real unknowns: live spot price and implied volatility. Shows fair value, all 4 Greeks, and an optional "market is pricing this rich/cheap" comparison if you paste your broker's mid price.
Best for
Sanity-checking a specific strike before you trade it — including strikes that came out of any other tool in the suite.
Open FairValue →
StrategyAI AI + Web Search
Jade Lizard / Broken Wing Butterfly, earnings-aware
▾
What it does
Earnings mode: exactly 2 strategies — Jade Lizard + Broken Wing Butterfly, sized for entry the day after the print. Non-Earnings mode: broader menu (BWB always included, plus whichever of Big Lizard / Ratio Spread / CSP fit). Strikes are anchored to the real expected move (ATM straddle × 0.85), and defined-risk legs are filtered so credit collected is always ≥40% of spread width. Optional GEX toggle adds a dealer-gamma read.
Best for
The most rigorous, rule-based strategy construction in the suite — use this when you want the math shown, not just a suggestion.
Open StrategyAI →
PortfolioAI AI + Vision
"What's your next move?" — adjustment plans, not new ideas
▾
What it does
Enter your current open positions manually, or upload a screenshot of your broker's positions screen (Claude's vision extracts them automatically). Pick an intent — roll, close, hedge, adjust — or let it auto-suggest one based on what it sees. Generates a specific adjustment plan for the positions you already hold.
Best for
You're already in a trade and need to decide what to do next — not looking for a new idea.
Open PortfolioAI →
MoonshotAI AI + Web Search
Stock candidates with credible 10x+ potential
▾
What it does
Set sector/theme, market-cap ceiling, and time horizon. Returns 5-7 real, currently-researched stock candidates with a specific catalyst thesis (not generic AI hype), risk tier, and what could go wrong. Save picks to a persistent watchlist that survives across visits.
Best for
Equity-level speculative ideas, not a single option's payoff — see Lottery tab for that instead.
Open MoonshotAI →
WeeklyAI AI + Live Data
Full weekly income plan, risk-tiered, saveable
▾
What it does
Capital presets, %/$ target toggle, three risk tiers (Moderate / Aggressive / Ultra), and a "macro event imminent" toggle that shifts the AI toward shorter DTE and wider strikes. Generates a full weekly plan grounded in live price data, with saved-plan history, PDF export, and share.
Best for
The most complete version of "give me this week's income trade" — more thorough than OptionsAI's Weekly Income tool.
Open WeeklyAI →
CompoundAI Math + AI
Reverse-engineer the rate you need, track progress
▾
What it does
Enter capital, target gain %, timeline, and a compounding period (1/2/4 weeks). Instantly computes the exact per-period return you'd need — no AI involved in the math — plus an honest reality-check tier (Conservative → Unrealistic) and a "one flat period" stress test. Each period can be checked in with real results (green/yellow/red), which automatically recalculates the required rate for whatever's left. AI is only used for the optional "find strategies for this rate" step, with live price verification against Finnhub.
Best for
Planning and tracking toward a specific dollar goal by a specific date — the only tool in the suite built around a target, not a single trade.
Open CompoundAI →
Inside GammaEdge (the 5 tabs on the main dashboard)
StratEdge Instant, No AI
Watchlist + instant P&L across 5 fixed strategies
▾
What it does
Pick a ticker from your watchlist, get instant computed P&L for Straddle, Strangle, Iron Condor, Bull Call, and Bear Put, with a "best fit" flagged automatically based on the actual dollar outcomes (not a lookup table). Includes VolSignal — a live IV-vs-RV read for any ticker with an AI commentary option.
vs StockAI
StratEdge is instant and formula-based (no AI wait); StockAI is AI-researched with live options chain and paper-trade tracking. Use StratEdge for a fast look, StockAI for a deeper one.
Backtest Instant, No AI
Historical performance, straddle or 45Δ/40Δ strangle
▾
What it does
Day-by-day and week-by-week historical backtest of buying an ATM straddle or a 45Δ/40Δ strangle (toggle between all three), sized by your capital. Capital basis is shown live at the top of results and is directly editable, not just buried in Settings.
Alloc Instant, No AI
Yield comparison for CSPs, covered calls, etc.
▾
What it does
Compares income-generating allocation strategies with metrics scoped per strategy — capital required, yield/cycle, breakeven cushion, max loss/downside floor.
Planner Instant, No AI
Weekly plan with position sizing + stop-loss
▾
What it does
Set capital, target, risk profile. "Position Size" slider controls how much capital goes into a trade; "Max Loss per Trade %" is a separate stop-loss trigger. The card now shows the compounded true dollar risk (position size % × stop-loss %) directly, since that combined number — not either slider alone — is what you're actually risking.
Lottery Instant, No AI
Single OTM option payoff table, Day 1 → Expiry
▾
What it does
Pick a ticker and a direction (Spike / Crash / Either), set OTM %, price, IV, DTE, capital. Returns a Black-Scholes payoff table across Day 1 / 2 / 3 / Expiry with a ~25% Day-1 IV crush baked in.
vs MoonshotAI
Different job entirely — this prices a single option's payoff for a move you already have in mind; MoonshotAI finds the stock candidate in the first place.
Worth Knowing
⚠️ Several tools do similar-sounding things on purpose
StockAI/StratEdge (single ticker), PortfolioAI/OptionsAI's audit (existing positions), and WeeklyAI/Alloc/OptionsAI's income tool (weekly income) all overlap in topic but differ in depth and mechanism (AI vs instant formula, adjustment vs audit). Each entry above notes the distinction — when in doubt, the AI-tagged tool is deeper/slower, the "Instant, No AI" tag is faster/lighter.
Shared keys, one setup. Every AI tool uses the same Anthropic key (ge_key in your browser's storage) — set it once in any tool's ⚙️ Settings and it works everywhere. Tools that pull live prices directly (StockAI, StrategyBuilder, FairValue, CompoundAI, WeeklyAI) also share a Finnhub key (ge_fh_key) for that — optional, but without it those tools fall back to AI web search for price, which can be slower and occasionally stale.
